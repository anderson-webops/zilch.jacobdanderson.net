# Zilch 1.3.0

## Multiple-aware Hard

- Standard Hard compares keeping a multiple, collecting extra scoring dice,
  banking, and taking hot dice together.
- Its decision accounts for the actual multiple, points at risk, score
  position, opening requirements, and available outright wins.
- The compact Tips page explains the new choices. Stealing keeps its separate
  strategy, and Easy and Medium retain their existing policies.
- The same refinement is integrated into the headless C++, Visual C++, and
  Java projects, including their paced turn flows.

The study retained 23.93 million distinct simulated games. In the primary
500,000-game fresh holdout, new Hard earned 51.4544% of match points against
v1.2.0 Hard (ties count as half a win; paired 95% interval 51.3338% to 51.5750%).
This is a tested advantage for the full feature pack, not proof of optimal play
or a claim that preserving a multiple is always best.

Website decisions match each companion implementation across 79,370 tested
states, including saved chains, endgame boundaries, and Stealing. The Visual
C++ port also corrects a saved multiple extended by three matching dice.

[Research and reproducible evidence](https://github.com/anderson-webops/zilch.jacobdanderson.net/blob/v1.3.0/docs/research/multiple-selection-2026-09/report.md)
