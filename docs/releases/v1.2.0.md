# Zilch 1.2.0

## Smarter Hard

- Standard Hard is more willing to roll six hot dice. Its six-dice base banking
  cutoff rises to 5,000, while opening and opponent-aware finish decisions still
  take priority.
- Once it decides to bank, Hard collects every remaining guaranteed scoring
  die without taking another roll. Collecting hot dice preserves that bank decision.
- Stealing retains its separate policy and scoring-selection behavior.
- Older saved computer turns refresh their selection when resumed.
- The compact strategy page reflects the refined policy.

The investigation retained 4.93 million simulated games. In the primary
500,000-game fresh holdout, the refined bot earned 51.5992% of match points
against the previous Hard (ties count as half a point). A separate holdout
also verified the six-dice change against collection-only Hard. These are
tested improvements, not a proof of optimal play.

The same strategy was integrated into the Java, Visual C++, and headless C++
projects. Website and simulator selections and banking decisions matched across
39,152 deterministic cases, including Stealing, saved multiples, and Final Chase.
